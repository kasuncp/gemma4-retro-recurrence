Figure 6 · Experiment 7

The cross-benchmark inversion.

Three benchmarks, three different stories. C2 dominates ARC-Challenge by 62 points. MATH floors at zero across every condition — a model-capability ceiling, not a prompt ceiling. Figure 8 · Experiment 9

The extractor ceiling.

Of C2's 500 GSM8K completions, the model reasons its way to the gold integer in 454 (90.8%). The original last-integer regex scores only 358 (71.6%) as correct — leaving 96 reasoning-correct answers on the floor. A prose-aware extractor recovers 32 of them at zero compute cost, lifting C2 to 78.0%.

Layer Count / 500 Implied accuracy Δ vs original
Original last-integer regex35871.6%—
Smart_v2 prose-aware extractor39078.0%+6.4 pp
Gold integer present in completion text45490.8%+19.2 pp

Smart_v2 extractor: prefer the last **bold** number, then Answer: N, then end-of-line = N, then last-integer fallback. Of the 26 problems where A3 (8-shot) is correct and C2 is scored wrong, 24 (92.3%) contain the gold integer in C2's completion text — they are extractor failures, not reasoning failures.
